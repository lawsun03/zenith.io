# MGC 2.5-Year Sweep — grade floors × fib gate (2026-06-10)

**Data:** `bars/bars_MGC_jan2024_jun2026.csv` (853k 1min bars, 2024-01-01 → 2026-06-08).
**Config:** live `bot_config.json` (faithful strategy_params path), `--no-risk-limits`,
post-fill-fix code (ca0b21f) + fixed fib metric (07d4410). Artifacts:
`backtests/sweep_minF_fib.json`, `sweep_minC.json`, `sweep_minB.json`.

## Grade-floor sweep (path-faithful runs)

| floor | entries | win% | profit factor | equity (from $50k) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F (live) | 6,899 | 28% | 0.91 | $7,051 (−$42.9k) |
| C | 2,642 | 36.9% | 0.84 | $19,275 (−$30.7k) |
| B | 461 | 40.9% | 0.78 | $40,578 (−$9.4k) |

Tighter floors raise win rate but **worsen profit factor** — the kept trades aren't
better, there are just fewer of them. Every floor, every year: negative.

Flat. Fib extension has **no discriminating power** at this sample size. The 06-08

The fib-gate idea is closed.

## Conclusion

The sweep-displacement→iFVG reversal strategy as configured has **negative expectancy
on MGC across 2.5 years under every tested configuration**. Avg win $95 vs avg loss
$51 at 28% WR needs ~35% WR to break even; no filter tested gets the *kept* trades
above water. Today's −$488 was the distribution, not variance.

Caveats that could move (not flip) these numbers: no HTF feed in backtest (grades
skew low; delivery/P-D criteria inactive), fixed 1-tick slippage, stale-FVG signals
included. None plausibly rescues PF 0.78–0.91.

**Implication:** stop tuning filters; the entry model itself needs rework (or the
strategy needs to be evaluated on the other instruments / with HTF feed before any
more live trading on MGC). Known artifact bug: per-trade rows double-count partial
exits and `stats.net_pnl` ≠ equity-curve delta — trust the equity curve.
