## Why 2022 Was the Straddle's Best Year

2022 was characterized by 40-year-high inflation. CPI prints frequently surprised to the upside, forcing the Fed into rapid consecutive rate hikes. Each CPI release produced:
- A unambiguous directional catalyst (hot CPI = sell NQ)
- Strong, sustained moves (no immediate reversal)
- Very few "multi-component uncertainty" reversals (unlike NFP's multi-number complexity)

Only 3 events whipsawed (Jan, Sep, Nov) — all produced strong spikes in both directions within the entry window, indicating genuine two-sided uncertainty at those specific releases.

The 9/12 events that hit 3R represent a particularly clean trading environment where inflation-driven rate expectations created unambiguous directional momentum.

### Key Observation: Anti-Correlation Benefit

The 6y base drops from $387→$305/mo (-21%) when 2022 is included — because 2022 was an iFVG/ORB drought year (the base engine struggled badly). But the CPI straddle partially offsets this:
- 6y with straddle: $596/mo vs 5y with straddle: $701/mo (only -15% degradation from including 2022)
- The straddle's strong 2022 performance (+$291/mo) cushions the base engine's worst year

This anti-correlation (straddle best when base engine worst) is a genuine portfolio benefit. High-inflation rate-hike regimes where discretionary momentum strategies lose are exactly when event-driven CPI straddling wins.

## Verdict

**CONFIRMED** — 2022 holdout supports CPI straddle deployment.

The 2022 result (PF 8.98) is the strongest of any calendar year tested:
- 2021/2023/2024/2025: combined PF 5.02 (1-min), 5.99 (1s)
- 2022: PF 8.98 (1-min only; 1s data unavailable for 2022)

The deployment decision (enabled live 2026-06-15) is validated by this retrospective holdout. The strategy is regime-resilient: it works well in both normal environments (PF 5+) and high-volatility inflation environments (PF 9+).

**Remaining gap:** The 2022 holdout used 1-min resolution (1s data was not fetched for 2022). The 1-min oracle understates the edge slightly (PF 5.02 vs 5.99 on 5y). The 2022 PF 8.98 figure could be modestly higher at 1s resolution. This gap is acceptable — the direction is strongly confirmatory and the cost of 2022 1s data (~$1-2) would not justify re-running.
