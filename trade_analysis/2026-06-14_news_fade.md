# News FADE (counter-trend) — raw profitability (complement to the straddle)

**Date:** 2026-06-14 · **Spec (screenshot playbook):** let algos clear (skip 1st min),
let the first ~15-min spike exhaust, enter COUNTER to the move on a reversal-close
confirmation, hard stop just beyond the initial news-candle extreme. TP tested: 1R, 2R,
mean-revert-to-ref. 5-min bars, MNQ, 2022 excluded, no lookahead.
Script: `scripts/fomc_fade.py`. (One mechanization of a discretionary idea; "exhaustion"
is fuzzy — finer/alt reversal rules could shift magnitudes.)

## Takeaways
1. **FOMC is untradeable at 5-min both ways.** The straddle loses (FOMC fakeout) AND the
   fade loses (reversals too inconsistent to clear a stop beyond the spike extreme). Skip it.
2. **CPI has real structure both ways** — the with-trend straddle is the stronger expression
   (PF ~5, +1.5R); the mean-revert fade is a secondary, smaller edge (PF 1.9, +0.36R, 5/5).
   They are alternative entries on the same event (would conflict if run together), not additive.
3. **PPI is junk both ways** — marginal straddle, negative fade.
4. Net: the news edge is **CPI-specific and direction-specific (the breakout)**. FOMC/PPI add
   nothing. This narrows the news work to "CPI breakout straddle" (pending tick confirm, B85).
