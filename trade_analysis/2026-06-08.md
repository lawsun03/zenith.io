## Two Dominant Failure Modes

**1. fib<1.0× on every A− entry.** 10/12 entries had a reversal body smaller than the manipulation bar's range — the grader records it but does not gate on it. The 0.63× entry at 02:53 ET is the extreme. The bot took a long string of the weakest passing setups.

**2. Adverse slippage → tightened stop → stopped on right side.** The bracket places the stop relative to the actual fill. MNQ filled +11.25pt above plan; several MES longs filled high. Excursion tracker shows multiple of these were `stopped_then_target` — direction correct, but slip-tightened stop got hit first.

---

## What the fib extension measures

```
fib_extension = displacement_bar.body_size / sweep_bar.full_range
```
- **Manipulation leg** = sweep bar's full high-to-low range
- **Reversal leg** = displacement bar body size
- `fib < 1.0×` → reversal body smaller than manipulation range → reversal is weaker than the trap that caused it → continuation risk

The grader threshold is 1.0×. Below that it logs `fib=low` but still passes A−. The gate doesn't exist yet.
